How to Use Lead Finder

Lead Finder helps you discover companies by industry and location, then find executive contacts with verified email patterns.

1. Search

  1. Enter an industry (e.g. "aerospace", "healthcare", "manufacturing")
  2. Enter a city in California (e.g. "Long Beach", "Los Angeles")
  3. Adjust radius (default 15 miles) and max results
  4. Click Search to find companies via Google Places

2. Enrich Contacts

  1. In the results, click Find Contacts on any company
  2. The system discovers executives via web scraping + LinkedIn search
  3. Contacts are validated before being saved to the database

3. Discover Email Patterns

  1. Go to Companies tab and click Discover to search for the company's email pattern
  2. Or click ✏️ Edit to manually enter a validated pattern (e.g. {first}.{last}@boeing.com)
  3. Mark source as "Validated via Search" or "Manual Entry"

4. Export & Use

  1. Click Get Emails to derive emails for all contacts using the known pattern
  2. Click Export Patterns to download a per-company CSV with all 21 pattern variations
  3. When pattern is known, the export shows a single validated email per contact
  4. When unknown, the export shows all 21 variations for manual validation

5. Browse & Manage

  1. Sessions — view all past searches and re-enrich stale data
  2. Companies — browse all discovered companies, filter by industry/city
  3. Contacts — view and edit individual contacts, remove irrelevant ones
  4. Audit — quality report showing contacts with missing data
